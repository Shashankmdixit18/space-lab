# Solar system texture credits

Surface maps, the Sun map, Earth's day/night/cloud maps, the Moon map, Venus atmosphere map, and Saturn ring map in this folder are from Solar System Scope.

- Creator: Solar System Scope / INOVE
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- Source and license details: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- Download catalog: https://sites.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- NASA imagery/data attribution: Solar System Scope states that its texture set is based in part on NASA imagery/data, including NASA Blue Marble for Earth. NASA's original public imagery remains credited to NASA; these compiled maps are redistributed under the Solar System Scope CC BY 4.0 terms.

Files prefixed `2k_` are the 2048 px source maps. Files prefixed `1k_` are locally downsampled derivatives for small screens and Lite mode (resized to half width and height, with no added source imagery). The scene loads the 1K variants on phones and in Lite mode.
